## 🌟 Overview

**Spoookify** is a modern, high-performance Android music streaming application built with **Jetpack Compose**, **Kotlin Coroutines**, **Hilt Dependency Injection**, and **ExoPlayer (Media3)**. Engineered by **MRD technologies**, Spoookify offers crystal-clear lossless audio playback without ads, mandatory logins, or tracking.

---

## ✨ Features

- ⚡ **100% Ad-Free Listening**: Stream your favorite songs uninterrupted without ads or video bloat.
- 🛡️ **Privacy-First Guest Mode**: Instant access to music without mandatory registration or email tracking.
- ☁️ **Firebase Cloud Sync**: Seamlessly sync custom playlists, liked tracks, and listening history with your Google Account.
- 🎧 **Lossless Audio Engine**: High-bitrate Opus/M4A streaming powered by ExoPlayer (Media3).
- 🎚️ **Equalizer & Smart Audio Insights**: Dynamic frequency EQ presets and real-time song analytics (BPM, Energy, Key).
- 🚀 **Offline Downloads**: Download tracks directly to your local device storage for offline playback anywhere.
- 🚫 **Pure Music Filtering**: Advanced title and channel filtering that eliminates non-music videos (trading tutorials, ads, vlogs).

---

## 📱 Tech Stack & Architecture

- **UI Framework**: Android Jetpack Compose (Material3 + Glassmorphism Theme)
- **Language**: Kotlin 100%
- **Architecture**: MVVM + Clean Architecture + Repository Pattern
- **Dependency Injection**: Dagger Hilt
- **Media Playback**: AndroidX Media3 (ExoPlayer)
- **Local Storage**: Room Database + DataStore Preferences
- **Backend & Sync**: Firebase Analytics & Cloud Firestore
- **Networking**: OkHttp3 + NewPipe Extractor
